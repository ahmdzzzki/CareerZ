## Description

Find a career direction worth exploring—and turn it into practical next steps.

CareerZ helps students and fresh graduates reflect on their interests, understand their current skills, and build evidence of what they can do.

EXPLORE SIX TECHNOLOGY CAREER PATHS
Software Engineering, Mobile Development, Product Design, Product Management, Data & Analytics, and AI & Machine Learning.

DISCOVER YOUR DIRECTION
Take CareerZ Compass to explore matches based on eight tendencies. See the traits behind each match, compare leading directions, or choose a path directly.

UNDERSTAND YOUR STARTING POINT
Assess eight skills in your selected career. Behavioral descriptions and evidence levels help you reflect on your experience. See a skill breakdown and the areas to focus on next.

BUILD WORK YOU CAN SHOW
Follow ten practical missions across Foundation, Practice, and Portfolio. Each mission explains the task, the evidence to create, and a reflection to help you learn from your decisions.

TRACK REAL PROGRESS
Save mission notes, complete or undo milestones, and revisit past assessments. Reassess to compare your progress within a career path. Changing direction keeps the work you have already recorded.

OFFLINE FIRST
Assessments, instructions, and progress work on your device without a login. Sign in with Apple is optional. Supported device builds use private iCloud synchronization to carry progress between devices. Optional further-reading links require internet.

CareerZ provides self-assessment and career exploration guidance for personal development. It is not a psychological assessment, professional certification, or guarantee of employment.

## Review notes draft

- CareerZ supports six career development paths with 48 skills and 60 practical missions.
- No CareerZ account or demo credentials are required. Reviewers can start from onboarding, choose a direction in Discover, and complete its skill assessment to open the roadmap.
- Mission completion requires a short evidence note and reflection. Notes are self-reported; the app does not verify external portfolio artifacts.
- Sign in with Apple is optional. iCloud synchronization uses the iCloud account configured on the device, independently of the Apple sign-in connection.
- All core content is bundled. Further reading opens external websites; it is optional.
