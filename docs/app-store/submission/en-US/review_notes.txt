CareerZ is a career exploration and self-development app for students and fresh graduates. No CareerZ account or demo credentials are required to use the core app. Leave "Sign-in required" unchecked.

Review flow:
1. Tap Get Started, open Discover, and either take CareerZ Compass or choose a career direction directly.
2. Choose any of the six paths, then complete its 16-step skill assessment (capability and evidence for eight skills).
3. Open the generated roadmap. Each path has ten missions across Foundation, Practice, and Portfolio.
4. Open the first unlocked mission, add an evidence note and reflection, then complete it. Progress is retained after relaunch.
5. Reassess within the same path to compare actual stored results. Changing career keeps earlier work.

Sign in with Apple is optional. CloudKit sync uses the device's iCloud account independently of the Apple sign-in connection. Assessments, bundled content, and local progress work offline. Further-reading links are optional and open external websites.

Profile provides Privacy Policy, Support, About Assessment, reset controls, and deletion of all progress. Mission evidence is self-reported. Compass matches are exploration guidance, not psychological testing, employability certification, or an employment guarantee.
