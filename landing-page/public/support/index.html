Contact CareerZ

Email ahzaki95@gmail.com for questions, bugs, accessibility feedback, or privacy requests. Include your app version, iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, and the steps that led to the issue. Please do not send passwords or verification codes.

Does CareerZ need an account?

No. Assessments, missions, and progress work locally without an account. Apple sign-in and iCloud sync are optional. External learning resources need internet.

Where is my progress?

Each career direction keeps its own assessment history and mission progress. Switch back to that direction to see its data. Changing direction does not erase previous work.

iCloud synchronization

Check that your device is signed in to iCloud and iCloud is enabled for CareerZ. Use the same iCloud account across devices, connect to the internet, and allow time for synchronization. The Profile screen reports available sync status. Apple sign-in alone does not switch the device iCloud account.

Resetting or deleting data

Open Profile to reset assessment history, reset roadmap progress, or delete all CareerZ progress. Read the confirmation carefully: deletion cannot be undone and applies to synchronized records too.

About the assessment

Compass suggests directions worth exploring; it is not a psychological diagnosis or probability of career success. Skill scores reflect your self-reported capability and evidence. CareerZ provides personal development guidance and does not guarantee employment or professional certification.
