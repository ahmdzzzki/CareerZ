Privacy Policy

Your career journey belongs to you.

Effective October 19, 2026

What CareerZ stores

CareerZ stores your selected career direction, Compass responses and results, skill assessment history, mission evidence notes, reflections, and completion dates on your device. Preferences such as onboarding completion are also saved locally.

Optional Apple services

You can use CareerZ without signing in. If you choose Sign in with Apple, CareerZ stores your Apple account identifier in the device Keychain. The app does not request your name or email address through Apple sign-in.

On supported device builds, progress can synchronize through the private CloudKit database associated with the iCloud account signed in on your device. This requires iCloud availability and network access. Sign in with Apple and your device iCloud account are separate services. Apple processes data used by these services under its own privacy policy.

No advertising or tracking

CareerZ has no advertising, third-party analytics SDK, tracking, or custom backend. We do not sell your assessment data. CareerZ does not send your responses to an AI service.

Your choices and deletion

Profile provides controls to reset assessment history, reset roadmap progress, and delete all CareerZ progress. Deleting progress also deletes the corresponding synchronized records when CloudKit synchronization is active; propagation depends on connectivity and Apple services. Disconnecting Apple sign-in removes the locally stored sign-in identifier and does not itself delete your progress or sign out of device iCloud. Removing the app alone may leave data in iCloud.

Resources and support

Optional learning links open third-party websites governed by their own policies. If you email support, we receive the address and information you choose to send. Avoid including sensitive assessment or personal details unless needed to resolve your request.

This website

This support website is hosted by Vercel, which may process standard request information such as IP address, browser details, and request time to provide hosting and security. We do not add analytics or advertising cookies.
