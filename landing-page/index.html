BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Good questions.
Honest answers.

Can I download CareerZ yet?

We’re preparing CareerZ for its first App Store release. The screens here are from the working app. We’ll add the download link when it is publicly available.

Will Compass choose my career for me?

No. Compass suggests directions worth exploring based on your answers and explains the traits behind each match. It is not a psychological assessment or a prediction of career success. The choice is yours.

What works without internet?

Compass, skill assessments, mission instructions, notes, and progress work locally. Optional iCloud sync, Apple sign-in, and links to further reading require a connection.

Do I need to pay or create an account?

CareerZ 1.0 has no subscriptions, in-app purchases, or required account. Apple sign-in is optional, and iCloud synchronization uses the iCloud account configured on your device.

What happens if I change direction?

Your assessments and mission work are kept for each direction. Switching back brings you back to that career’s history. Scores from different careers are not presented as a growth trend.
